# DigiRoute — Turn Any Doorstep Into a Precise 10-Character Sovereign DIGIPIN

## 🚀 The Launch Post (X / Twitter / LinkedIn)

Addresses in India are complicated. Finding the exact door is a nightmare.

DigiRoute solves this by transforming imprecise GPS coordinates into a 10-character sovereign micro-address (`39J-M99-P9CJ`).

# Hyperframes Composition Brief: DigiRoute 60-Second Showcase

## Objective
Create a high-impact, 60-second launch showcase video for DigiRoute adhering strictly to the "Refined Utilitarian" design system, dark mode aesthetic, fast-paced transitions, and 5 distinct scenes.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
- Duration: 60.0 seconds

## Design System & Theme
- **Vibe**: Premium, fast-paced, and minimalist ('Refined Utilitarian')
- **Colors**: Dark mode background (Zinc `#09090b`) with energetic Orange/Amber highlights (`#f97316` / `#fb923c`)
- **UI Elements**: Sharp edges (4px border-radius), clean sans-serif typography (`Geist`, `Inter`), `Geist Mono` for DIGIPIN codes, zero cluttered graphics

## Storyboard & Timing (60.0s Total)
1. **Scene 1 (0.0s – 10.0s | 10.0s)**: The Hook & Problem (Fast typing on dark screen -> street zoom -> glowing DigiRoute amber logo -> sovereign DIGIPIN)
2. **Scene 2 (10.0s – 28.0s | 18.0s)**: The 5-Step Creation Flow (Rapid cuts across Step 1 Base Location, Step 2 Visual Lock, Step 3 Entrance Pin, Step 4 Details & Security, Step 5 Review/Live Micro-Address)
3. **Scene 3 (28.0s – 40.0s | 12.0s)**: Security & QR Codes (Digital address card 3D flip to printable QR badge + Dashboard Address Book with expiring guest links)
4. **Scene 4 (40.0s – 50.0s | 10.0s)**: The Native Android App (3D phone mockup showing high-tech scanner HUD + offline India route planning map)
5. **Scene 5 (50.0s – 60.0s | 10.0s)**: The Future Roadmap & CTA (Interactive Emergency SOS & live radar tracking from codebase + final brand outro CTA `digiroute.antideploy.com/download`)

## Audio & Music
- Background Music: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (60.0s duration, volume 0.35, smooth fade-out 58s-60s)
- Sound Effects: Multi-track SFX layer synced to keystrokes, step transitions, card flips, scanner sweeps, and brand chimes.
- Captions: High-contrast, dynamic subtitles positioned in the lower third with amber keyword highlights.
